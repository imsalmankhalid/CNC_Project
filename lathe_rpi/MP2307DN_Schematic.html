⚡ Key Specifications
Input: 4.75V – 23V (12V nominal)
Output: 5.0V fixed (±1%)
Max current: 3.0A
Frequency: ~550kHz (R24=22kΩ)
Efficiency: ~88% at 12V→5V/2A
Quiescent current: 1.2mA
Shutdown current: 0.1µA (EN=GND)
🔧 Pin Function Notes
VIN — Unregulated 12V input (after fuse)
EN — Enable: HIGH=on, LOW=shutdown. Tie to 3V3 via 100kΩ.
SW — Switch node: connects to inductor. NOISY — keep trace short!
BOOT — Bootstrap: 100nF cap between BOOT and SW.
RT/CT — Timing resistor to GND sets switching frequency.
FB — Feedback: tied to GND for fixed 5V output.
GND — Pins 4 & 8: connect to solid GND plane.
